The Verse Method · shown on Rooted Sanctum

  1. Explore

    Every direction on the table first: drinks, merch, signage, type, colour. A real range, not one idea polished.

  2. Curate

    We narrow it together. The mark is tested solid, outlined and in colour until one earns its place.

  3. Craft

    The chosen direction is finished by hand and carried everywhere: neon, apparel, packaging, the room itself.
